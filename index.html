About

I currently work at Anyverse Dynamics in Shanghai.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), first at the School of EEE under the supervision of Prof. Xie Lihua, and subsequently at the School of MAE advised by Prof. Mir Feroskhan. I received my B.Eng., M.Eng., and Ph.D. degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT).

My current research focuses on embodied AI, including robot imitation learning, predictive representations, and egocentric demonstration capture. I am interested in connecting visual perception and SLAM with data collection and robot learning. My doctoral research focused on SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping).

Open Source

JEPA Policy

Diffusion-free robot imitation learning through paired action and future representation prediction. A shared Transformer jointly predicts action chunks and future visual representations in two feed-forward steps.

Paper · Code · Project Page

UpDown-SC

Gravity-canonicalized dual-envelope Scan Context for indoor LiDAR place recognition. The release includes a standalone C++ implementation, baseline evaluation protocols, and reproducibility artifacts.

Paper · Code · Project Page

LIO Gravity Estimation

A controlled study of online gravity and accelerometer-bias estimation in LiDAR-inertial odometry.
